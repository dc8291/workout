Ankle R (cm)
Ankle test (knee-to-wall): face a wall with one foot forward, toes straight. Bend that knee to touch the wall while the heel stays flat. Slide the foot back until you can only just do it, then measure toe-to-wall in cm. Test both feet. Bigger is better; about 10 cm or more is a common target.
